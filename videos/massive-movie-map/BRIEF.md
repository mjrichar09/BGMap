## Intent

A 45-second launch post for X for Massive Movie Map (https://massive-movie-map.mjrfx.com),
a site that places 36,841 films by what their plots say. Show the real site and sell the
idea: an AI read every plot, drew a map of cinema, and found things on its own. Close on
"go get lost" and the URL. Full long-form plan: "Movie Map Demo Video — Plan.md" in the repo root.

Beats:

1. 0–6s: projector dust settles into the cloud. "36,841 films, placed by their plots."
2. 6–15s: search The Godfather; neighbors fan out (Part II 0.96, Part III 0.93, Gotti 0.88).
3. 15–23s: quick diagram, plot → numbers → map.
4. 23–35s: found on its own. The Lion King 1994 ↔ 2019 = 1.00; Pokémon the Movie 2000 is
   the least original film on the map (originality 0.07); an unmade movie in an empty gap
   (Second Unit: a film crew shooting a tsunami movie gets a real tsunami).
5. 35–45s: "Go get lost." URL and credits.

## Customizations

- AI-generated voiceover, about 110 words; offer 2–3 voice options after the storyboard.
- Captions burned in.
- Quiet music bed with projector-click accents.
- Look: the site's own. Late Show (near-black #0b0807, amber #ffb547) as the base, Matinee

  Oswald, Courier Prime, Work Sans.
