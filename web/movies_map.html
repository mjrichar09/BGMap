        Unmade movies gaps in the space

        Places between two kinds of film where nothing has been made yet. Found by blending every pair of clusters in the model's full space, not on this map, and keeping the blends that are emptiest compared with similar pairs. Each pitch was written by AI and placed by the same model as every film, to check it really lands in the gap.

          Film deserts: start from a film

          Pick a film and see the emptiest places between it and other kinds of film: blends of its plot with a nearby cluster that nothing on the map comes close to. Computed ahead of time in the model's full space, with no AI running live. Some deserts have a pitch written by AI (marked ✎), checked the same way as the ones above.
